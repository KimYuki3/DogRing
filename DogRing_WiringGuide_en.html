Cautions

Before connecting the battery

  • Keep the battery disconnected while wiring. If battery + and − touch, it can overheat or catch fire.
  • Connect the LED strip GND (white wire) only to D4184 OUT−. Never connect it directly to battery − or XIAO GND.
  • D4184 terminal names vary by product (DC+ / DC− / LOAD+ / LOAD−; TRIG may be labeled PWM or SIG).
  • Check the BAT+ / BAT− positions on the back of the XIAO by the labels printed on the board.
  • The DIN end of the LED strip is where the arrows start. Wired backwards, it will not light.
  • When done, connect the battery and hold the button for 1.5 s. If the LED strip flashes green once, the wiring is good.
